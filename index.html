Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Families & Creators

Everything you need to know about permanent software ownership, drive safety, and zero-cloud privacy.

Will Media Chronicle delete, move, or modify my original photos? +
Absolutely not. Media Chronicle operates strictly with read-only scanning permissions. Your photos remain exactly where they are on your external hard drives and SSDs. We never alter file timestamps, move directories, or compress original photos.
Do I need an active internet connection to use it? +
No. Media Chronicle is 100% offline and air-gapped. You can disconnect your PC from Wi-Fi completely, and all thumbnail generation, metadata searching, and local family grouping will work at full speed.
What happens if I unplug my external hard drive? +
Media Chronicle safely caches lightweight preview thumbnails and metadata index records locally. If your drive is disconnected, you can still search your library and see previews. Once you plug the drive back in, high-resolution original viewing is instantly restored.
How does the $79 Lifetime License work? +
It is a single, one-time payment. You receive a perpetual desktop license key valid for all v1.x releases on up to 3 personal Windows computers in your household. No recurring monthly or annual charges ever.
