Privacy Policy

Your family memories are sacred. Media Chronicle was deliberately founded on the absolute principle that personal photo archives should never be stored, indexed, or viewed on corporate cloud servers.

The Zero-Cloud Invariant

The Media Chronicle desktop application operates 100% offline. It never creates network sockets for photos, never transmits facial biometrics, does not log searches, and sends zero analytics telemetry to our team or any third party.

1. The Desktop Application: Complete Local Privacy

When you install and execute Media Chronicle on your Windows workstation:

  • In-Place Drive Access: Media Chronicle inspects external hard drives, SSDs, SD cards, and USB thumb drives in place. It reads image headers and EXIF metadata without modifying or duplicating your files.
  • Local Face Recognition & Biometrics: Biometric embeddings, face detection boxes, and family clusters are generated strictly on your local CPU/GPU using on-device models. Embeddings are stored in your local SQLite catalog on your machine. They are never transmitted over the internet.
  • Zero Telemetry & Tracking: The application contains no crash reporters, analytics SDKs, session recordings, or phone-home beacons. It functions identically whether your computer is connected to the internet or completely unplugged.

2. Website Information We Collect

Our public website (media-chronicle.akshatdhaundiyal.com) collects only the minimum necessary data to provide license delivery and customer support:

  • Email Address: When you purchase a license or request download access, we collect your email address strictly for:
    1. Delivering your permanent license key and receipt.
    2. Providing critical security updates or bugfix release notes.
    3. Responding to customer support or license recovery requests.
  • Standard Web Server Logs: Standard web server requests (e.g. IP address, user-agent string, page visited) may be processed temporarily by Cloudflare for DDoS protection and static asset delivery. No personal tracking profiles are constructed.

3. Payment Processing via Dodo Payments (Merchant of Record)

Payment Security Standard

We do not store or process your sensitive credit card or banking details. All checkout operations are managed by Dodo Payments Inc., acting as the authorized Merchant of Record.

Dodo Payments adheres to strict global payment security standards, including Level 1 PCI-DSS compliance. When you complete checkout, your payment information is encrypted and transmitted directly to Dodo Payments. Please consult the Dodo Payments Privacy Policy for full details on their regulatory and security controls.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use advertising trackers, retargeting pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads tags), or behavioral tracking scripts. We utilize only standard local storage / session storage mechanisms in your browser to maintain checkout continuity and user interface preferences.

5. How We Protect Your Data

We implement robust administrative, technical, and cryptographic measures:

  • All web communications occur over 256-bit TLS/SSL encrypted channels.
  • License generation keys utilize public-key asymmetric cryptography, verifiable completely offline by the desktop application.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your email address or customer records with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your physical location, we honor complete data autonomy:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the transactional records associated with your purchase email.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your email from our support and notification database at any time. (Note: Retaining a cryptographic record of your license key receipt may be necessary to reissue keys upon hardware upgrades).
  • Right to Rectification: You may update your contact email by contacting support.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. Media Chronicle is designed for adults and parents managing family archival hardware. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
